=== P794.S0 ===
Underfitting happens when a model is too simple to capture the underlying patterns in the data.
=== P794.S1 ===
It results in poor performance on both the training set and new data because it fails to learn adequately.
=== P795.S0 ===
Example: Fitting a straight line to data that clearly follows a more complex pattern, such as housing prices that increase then flatten out.
=== P795.S1 ===

=== P798.S0 ===
The ideal model strikes a balance between underfitting and overfitting.
=== P798.S1 ===

=== P799.S0 ===
Example: Using a quadratic model that fits the training data well and generalizes to unseen data.
